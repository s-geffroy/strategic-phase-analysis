# Scientific Publication Explanation

## Proposed title

**Strategic Phase Analysis: A Heuristic Framework for Diagnosing Transformation Regimes in International Systems**

## Core claim

Strategic Phase Analysis is a heuristic diagnostic framework designed to identify transformation regimes in international systems. It classifies cases by dominant phase, secondary phases, modifiers, transition signals, evidence status and analyst validation.

## Methodological positioning

The method is not a physical model of international relations. It uses a controlled analogy to structure reasoning about transformation, hardening, resilience and rupture.

The method is useful only if it remains:
- explicit in definitions ;
- traceable in evidence ;
- open to contradiction ;
- validated by human analysts ;
- limited in its claims.

## Academic contribution

The method contributes a disciplined vocabulary for cases where conventional labels such as “stable,” “unstable,” “escalating,” or “de-escalating” are insufficient.

It focuses on:
- regimes of transformation ;
- option closure ;
- resilience after shock ;
- coercive hardening ;
- layered interdependence ;
- transition watches.

## Limits to state explicitly

- The analogy is heuristic, not deterministic.
- Scores are support indicators, not measurements of physical quantities.
- Phase attribution remains judgment-guided and human-validated.
- Evidence quality varies across cases.
- Active crises require freshness checks.
- The method does not replace historical knowledge or area expertise.

## Annex logic

The full internal conceptual correspondence may be placed in a methodological annex. It should not dominate the article.
